Gemstone Rhapsody

Merge gems, clear the board, keep what you earn. An opera house to rebuild, instruments to collect, a new city when you are ready.

What it is

A merge puzzle for iPhone, set inside an opera house that burnt in 1945. Every run you play rebuilds something.

How it plays

Gems drop onto the stage. Two of the same kind touch and become one bigger stone, climbing from Ruby up to the Mega Diamond. Every gem carries a number of seconds on its face: merge it yourself and it adds them, let it fall and it takes them away. Let a stone cross the danger line and the run is over.

Underneath the ordinary stones sits the Enigma, a black diamond that will not merge with anything. You break it by landing gems against it, and when it goes the Polar Star rises.

What the playing pays for

Clearing a stage puts one piece of the house back. The floor first, then the front stalls, the curtain, the chandelier — twenty pieces in all, and the restoration screen names the next one before you have earned it.

The house is rebuilt by playing and by nothing else. None of it is for sale.

Eighty stages in Vienna, each with its own task: a score to beat, a chain to hold, a stone to forge, a minute to survive. More cities wait beyond Vienna.
