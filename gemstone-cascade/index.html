Gemstone Rhapsody

A merge puzzle set in an opera house. Drop gems, merge matching pairs — and every merge strikes a real orchestral note.

The game

Gemstone Rhapsody turns the classic merge puzzle into a night at the opera. Matching stones fuse into ever-rarer ones, climbing a ladder from Ruby to the dazzling Polar Star — and the black diamond, the Enigma, must be broken before the finale is yours.

Every run earns Notes, and Notes restore a gilded concert hall of hand-illustrated instruments and treasures. The hall begins dark. You light it back up, room by room, as you play.

What it is

  • Easy to learn, with endless depth. One tap drops a gem. The difficulty is in where.
  • A living Conservatory to restore. Earned by playing, never sold.
  • A classical soundtrack that drives every performance. The music is the game, not the wallpaper.
  • Free to play, with optional in-app purchases. Nothing is ever required to buy.
