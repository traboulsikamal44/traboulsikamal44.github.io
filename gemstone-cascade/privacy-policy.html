Privacy Policy

Gemstone Rhapsody (formerly Gemstone Cascade) · Last updated: July 13, 2026

Gemstone Rhapsody is a free game. Your progress stays on your device. The app shows optional, player-initiated rewarded ads through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases processed by Apple. We — Ludicrous Studio — run no servers and hold no account data about you.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device only (Apple's on-device storage / Capacitor Preferences) and is never transmitted to us:

  • Scores, game progress, and unlocked content
  • Notes (in-game currency), Conservatory restorations, Ovations, and World Tour progress
  • Purchase grants (what your bundles unlocked)
  • Settings and preferences

You can erase all of this at any time in-app: Settings (⚙) → Delete my data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gemstone Rhapsody uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, a "rescue" continue or a score doubler). Ads only play when you choose to watch one. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking (see below)
  • IP address and coarse (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks) and diagnostics

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies: see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob apps.

App Tracking Transparency

On first relevant use, iOS shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you tap Allow, the IDFA may be used to show more relevant (personalized) ads. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not used and you receive non-personalized ads instead — the game is identical either way. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The app also supports Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork framework for ad attribution.

In-App Purchases

Gemstone Rhapsody offers optional consumable bundles of Notes (in-game currency spent only on cosmetic content — never gameplay advantage). All transactions are processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see, handle, or store your payment or billing information. Apple provides us no personally identifying purchase data. Transactions are governed by Apple's Media Services Terms.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration, no email, no name, no contacts
  • No precise location
  • No photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No messages or social data
  • We operate no backend servers — gameplay data never leaves your device

Children's Privacy

Gemstone Rhapsody is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is delivered by Google AdMob in accordance with its policies, and users who decline tracking receive only non-personalized ads. If you believe a child's data has been collected improperly, contact us and we will work with our ad partner to address it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests concern data held by Google — see Google's My Ad Center and privacy controls. For anything on your device, use Settings (⚙) → Delete my data in-app, or contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes (for example, adding a new SDK) will be reflected here before they ship.
