Privacy Policy

Gemstone Rhapsody (formerly Gemstone Cascade) · Last updated: September 2, 2026

Gemstone Rhapsody is a free game. Your progress stays on your device. The app shows optional, player-initiated rewarded ads through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases processed by Apple. We — Serenza — run no servers and hold no account data about you.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device only (Apple's on-device storage / Capacitor Preferences) and is never transmitted to us:

  • Scores, game progress, and unlocked content
  • Notes (in-game currency), Conservatory restorations, Ovations, and World Tour progress
  • Purchase grants (what your bundles unlocked)
  • Settings and preferences

You can erase all of this at any time in-app: MY PROFILE → LEGAL → Delete my data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gemstone Rhapsody uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, a "rescue" continue or a score doubler). Ads only play when you choose to watch one. To serve, cap and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • A device identifier, used to deliver, cap and measure ads
  • IP address and coarse (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks) and diagnostics

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies: see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob apps.

Advertising identifiers and tracking

The app asks for your permission to track, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. About thirty seconds after launch, with no action from you, the background advert preload raises that prompt — so it can appear on its own, including while a game is in progress. If you tap through to a rewarded advert before then, the prompt is raised at that point instead.

If you allow tracking, Google AdMob may use Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to serve personalised rewarded adverts. If you decline, or have not yet answered, the Advertising Identifier is not used and every advert you are shown is non-personalised. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Whatever you choose, the Game itself does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for its own purposes, and we receive no advertising identifier ourselves. The app also supports Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork framework for ad attribution.

Analytics

The app includes the GameAnalytics SDK to measure how the game is played in aggregate — sessions, progression, and whether players return. It generates its own random identifier stored on your device and never reads Apple's Advertising Identifier. It receives no name, no email, no contact details, and nothing that identifies you personally.

In-App Purchases

Gemstone Rhapsody offers optional consumable bundles of Notes (in-game currency spent on in-game content and on items that assist a run). All transactions are processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see, handle, or store your payment or billing information. Apple provides us no personally identifying purchase data. Transactions are governed by Apple's Media Services Terms.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration, no email, no name, no contacts
  • No precise location
  • No photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No messages or social data
  • We operate no backend servers of our own. Gameplay data stays on your device, apart from anonymous aggregate statistics described above.

Children's Privacy

Gemstone Rhapsody is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is delivered by Google AdMob in accordance with its policies, and adverts are non-personalised for every player who has not allowed tracking. If you believe a child's data has been collected improperly, contact us and we will work with our ad partner to address it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests concern data held by Google — see Google's My Ad Center and privacy controls. For anything on your device, use MY PROFILE → LEGAL → Delete my data in-app, or contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes (for example, adding a new SDK) will be reflected here before they ship.
