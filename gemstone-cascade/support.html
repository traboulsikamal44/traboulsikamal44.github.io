Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore purchases?
Tap the ⚙ gear on the main menu, then "Restore Purchases".

What are Notes and what do they buy?
Notes are the in-game currency you earn by playing (or buy in optional bundles at the Box Office). They're spent only on cosmetic content — restoring instruments and treasures in your Conservatory. Nothing you can buy affects gameplay.

An ad didn't grant my reward — what do I do?
Rewarded ads need a network connection. If a reward didn't arrive, restart the app and try again; if it keeps happening, email us with your device model and iOS version.

How do I delete my data?
Tap the ⚙ gear on the main menu, then "Delete my data". This erases all local progress permanently.
